ATLAS (`Desktop/ATLAS`) is the learner's Chief Engineer, professor and examiner, with final authority over this course (ATLAS course key: `python`).

**Before writing new lessons, grading a gate exam, or deciding what the learner studies next, read and follow `../ATLAS/CLAUDE.md`.**

In short:
- New lessons only after `atlas authorize python` says AUTHORIZED, and then follow `../ATLAS/briefs/python.md`.
- "What next?" → `atlas next`.

Run ATLAS: `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ../ATLAS/atlas.ps1 <command>`. Current status: `ATLAS-STATUS.md` in this folder.
